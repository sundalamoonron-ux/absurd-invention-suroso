# Candidates — One-Sentence Absurd Invention Pitch
Event: u-suroso-69-one-sentence-absurd-invention-pitch-5326c34a ($2.50, top 10 paid)
Judge criteria (in order): 1) on-brief (ridiculous invention fixing a tiny everyday annoyance),
2) funniness from the invention itself, 3) cleverness of the fix (silly yet weirdly plausible),
4) originality, 5) tightness (one clean sentence: annoyance + invention + silly mechanism).

All candidates are one sentence, UTF-8, 1–220 chars. Lengths verified by script.

## C1 — Toaster parachute (148 chars)
A toaster that deploys a tiny parachute on every slice, so your toast floats gently onto the plate instead of landing butter-side down on the floor.

## C2 — Polite fridge light (130 chars)
A fridge light that dims itself politely at 3 a.m. so it never catches you mid-midnight-snack and judges you with full brightness.

## C3 — Self-locating remote (135 chars)
A remote control that beeps and flashes its own location whenever you pat the sofa cushions twice, ending the daily cushion excavation.

## C4 — Disappointed microwave (129 chars)
A microwave that sighs and counts down out loud in a disappointed voice every time you reheat the same coffee for the third time.

## C5 — Apologetic printer (139 chars)
A printer that prints one perfect page and a tiny apology note before jamming, so you forgive it instead of threatening it with the window.

## C6 — Eavesdropping shower (131 chars)
A shower head that eavesdrops on your alarm and pre-warms itself, so the first splash of the morning is never an ice-cold betrayal.

## C7 — Texting houseplant (121 chars)
A houseplant that texts you "I am fine, stop watering me" before you can drown it with love for the third time this week.

## C8 — Anti-fridge chair (123 chars)
A desk chair that gently rolls you back to your keyboard every time you drift toward the fridge for the fourth quick snack.

## C9 — Self-rebooting router (104 chars)
A Wi-Fi router that sighs audibly and reboots itself the moment it senses you standing up to yell at it.

## C10 — Passive-aggressive toothpaste (133 chars)
A toothpaste tube that squeezes itself from the bottom and leaves a tiny passive-aggressive note when you squeeze it from the middle.
